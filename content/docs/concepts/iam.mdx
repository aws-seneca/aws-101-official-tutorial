## In the workshop

The workshop kept IAM out of the hands-on to save time. You signed in as your account's user; the app used a database password, not AWS credentials.

## What this page will cover

- The root user, and why you should not use it day to day
- Users, groups, roles, and policies
- Roles for EC2: giving an instance permissions without storing keys on it
- Least privilege, with a real policy as the example
- MFA and the account security basics
