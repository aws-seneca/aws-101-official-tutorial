## Which kind of database

| You have | Reach for | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Rows with a fixed shape, joins between tables, SQL | A relational database: Amazon RDS (MySQL, PostgreSQL and others) or Amazon Aurora | [Relational](/docs/concepts/databases/relational) |
| Lookups by a known key, very high traffic, a flexible shape | A NoSQL database: Amazon DynamoDB | [NoSQL](/docs/concepts/databases/nosql) |
| The same reads over and over, and the database is the bottleneck | A cache in front of the database: Amazon ElastiCache | [Caching](/docs/concepts/databases/caching) |

For the sign-up app in the lessons, relational is the natural first choice: the data is a small table with a fixed shape, and SQL is what most people learn first. The other two matter because every design review asks the same question: what shape is the data, and how is it read?
