Amazon CloudWatch collects what your resources are doing and lets you act on it.

- **Metrics**: numbers over time. Many AWS services send them automatically: EC2 CPU, RDS free storage, load balancer request counts. You can publish your own.
- **Logs**: text output from apps and services, searchable in CloudWatch Logs.
- **Alarms**: watch a metric against a threshold. When it trips, notify someone (through SNS) or take an action, such as scaling an Auto Scaling group.
- **Dashboards**: metrics and logs on one screen. CloudWatch builds automatic ones for many services.

## A first alarm worth setting

A billing alarm, or an AWS Budget, so a forgotten instance cannot surprise you. See [Costs and the free plan](/docs/concepts/costs-and-free-plan).

## In the lessons

Open your instance in the EC2 console and choose the **Monitoring** tab: those graphs are CloudWatch metrics.
