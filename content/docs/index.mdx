## Who it is for

Seneca students with no AWS experience. You need a laptop and an AWS account. Curiosity is the only credential.

## Status of this guide

The **workshop** pages are complete. The **concepts** and **architecture** pages are outlines: the headings say what each page will cover, and club members are filling them in. Want to write one? See [Contributing](/docs/contributing).

The app you deploy is [aws-seneca/aws-101-workshop](https://github.com/aws-seneca/aws-101-workshop).
