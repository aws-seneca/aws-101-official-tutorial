description: Everything on one EC2 instance. Where the workshop starts.

```text
Browser ──HTTP──> EC2 instance
                   ├─ the app (Next.js)
                   └─ signups.json on the instance's disk
```

This is the workshop's Part 1.

## What works

- Simple: one thing to launch, one thing to look at.
- Cheap, and fine for a demo.

## What breaks

- **The data dies with the instance.** Terminate it, or lose its disk, and every sign-up is gone.
- **It cannot scale out.** A second instance would have its own `signups.json`, so the two would disagree.
- **Concurrent writes can clash.** Two sign-ups at the same moment can overwrite each other.

Design 2 fixes the first two by moving the data off the server.
